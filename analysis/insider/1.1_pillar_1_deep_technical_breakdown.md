## 1. Executive Purpose & Operational Defense Context

### 1.1 What is the Purpose of Pillar 1?
Medium Altitude Long Endurance (MALE) Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) deployed by the Indian Armed Forces carry out continuous strategic Intelligence, Surveillance, and Reconnaissance (ISR), border patrol, and target tracking missions lasting **18 to 30+ continuous hours**.

Unlike twin-engine military aircraft or commercial airliners that have redundant powerplants to divert safely if one engine fails, MALE UAVs rely on a **single 4-cylinder aero piston engine**.
* If a dry-sump scavenge pump, fuel injector nozzle, ignition coil, or cylinder head experiences critical degradation mid-sortie over contested or remote terrain (e.g., the Line of Actual Control in Ladakh or the Thar Desert), **the aircraft cannot glide back to base and is permanently lost**.
* Propulsion reliability is the single highest determinant of UAV mission survivability.

**Pillar 1 establishes an automated, mathematically grounded pre-flight gate.** It evaluates the propulsion unit's current physical health indices, thermodynamic baseline, and projected Remaining Useful Life (RUL) against the planned sortie duration **before takeoff clearance is granted**.

```
                        PILLAR 1: PRE-FLIGHT CERTIFICATION GATE
┌─────────────────────────┐      ┌───────────────────────────┐      ┌─────────────────────────┐
│ Planned Sortie Duration │ vs.  │ Probabilistic RUL (p10)   │ ──►  │ Automated Decision:     │
│ e.g., 18.0 Flight Hours │      │ e.g., 13.5 Hours on Pump  │      │ [NO-GO / ABORT SORTIE]  │
└─────────────────────────┘      └───────────────────────────┘      └─────────────────────────┘
```

### 1.2 DRDO Problem Statement (PS-26054) Requirements Addressed
1. **Autonomous Feasibility Certification:** Replaces static calendar or fixed-hour maintenance with true wear-based, condition-driven pre-flight validation.
2. **Conservative Statistical Safety Bounds (p10 RUL):** Computes conservative Remaining Useful Life bounds rather than optimistic averages to ensure a certified safety margin (minimum 2.0-hour safety buffer).
3. **Multi-Subsystem Health Interrogation:** Simultaneously checks all 6 core engine subsystems before takeoff:
   * Cylinder Head Assemblies (Thermal stress & baffle integrity)
   * Fuel Injection Rail (Pressure & injector nozzle balance)
   * Dual Ignition Harness (Coil dwell & misfire jitter)
   * Lubrication Circuit (Dry-sump pressure & oil viscosity)
   * Reduction Gearbox (Overload dog-clutch 3rd harmonic vibration)
   * Alternator Electrical Bus (Regulator voltage sag & battery current draw)
4. **Sensor Discrimination Pre-Check:** Prevents sensor disconnects, open-circuit thermocouples, or frozen ADC converters from triggering false engine teardown alarms.

### 1.3 Engineering Assumptions & Operating Constraints
* **100% Air-Gapped Execution:** All physics models, curve fitting, Monte Carlo simulations, and sensor sanity routines execute entirely on local ground station hardware without external cloud access.
* **Deterministic Execution Budget:** The telemetry and physics baseline updates at **20 Hz (< 50 ms per tick)**. Background degradation trend analysis and Monte Carlo RUL sampling execute asynchronously on a **20-second cycle (< 10 ms execution time)**.
* **Environmental Extremes:**
  * **Northern Sector (Ladakh):** High density altitude (11,000 to 25,000 ft MSL), sub-zero ambient temperatures (-28 °C to -12 °C), thin air (reduced cylinder cooling airflow, lower air density).
  * **Western Sector (Thar Desert):** Low altitude (sea level to 6,000 ft MSL), extreme ambient heat (+40 °C to +48 °C), severe thermal degradation of oil viscosity.

## 3. Processing, Calculations & 1D Thermodynamic Physics Twin

The core of Pillar 1 is the **1D Thermodynamic Virtual Physics Shadow** (`RotaxThermoModel` in `backend/physics/thermo_model.py`). It calculates what every parameter should be under nominal physical conditions given ambient altitude, OAT, throttle command, and airspeed.

```

└───────────────────────┘      └────────────────────────┘      └─────────────────────────┘
```

### 3.1 Mathematical Physics Formulation

#### 1. International Standard Atmosphere (ISA) & Density Derating
Given altitude in meters:
`alt_m = altitude_ft * 0.3048`  
`T_kelvin = max(200.0, OAT_C + 273.15)`

Barometric ambient pressure:
`P_ambient = max(10.0, 101.325 * (1.0 - (0.0065 * alt_m / 288.15)) ^ 5.25588) [kPa]`

Ambient air density:
`rho_ambient = (P_ambient * 1000.0) / (287.05 * T_kelvin) [kg/m^3]`  
`Density_Ratio = rho_ambient / 1.225`

#### 2. Induction Manifold Absolute Pressure (MAP) & Air Mass Flow
Normalized throttle opening:
`throttle_norm = max(0.0, min(1.0, TPS / 100.0))`

Manifold Absolute Pressure:
`MAP_expected = min(P_ambient, max(30.0, 35.0 + (P_ambient - 38.0) * (throttle_norm ^ 0.85))) [kPa]`

Volumetric efficiency:
`volumetric_efficiency = 0.82 + 0.08 * (throttle_norm ^ 0.5)`

Air mass flow rate:
`Displacement = 1352.0 * 1e-6 [m^3]`  
`air_mass_flow = Displacement * (RPM / 120.0) * rho_ambient * volumetric_efficiency * 3600.0 [kg/hr]`

#### 3. Fuel Mass Flow & Target Air-Fuel Ratio (AFR)
Target AFR ranges from stoichiometric (14.7) at loiter cruise to rich (12.8) at full takeoff power:
`target_AFR = 14.7 - 1.9 * (throttle_norm ^ 2.0)`  
`fuel_mass_flow_kg_hr = air_mass_flow / target_AFR`  
`fuel_density = 0.74 [kg/L]` (Avgas / Mogas)  
`fuel_flow_expected = max(4.0, fuel_mass_flow_kg_hr / fuel_density) [L/hr]`

#### 4. Thermal Equilibrium & Cylinder Head Temperature (CHT)
Heat generated is proportional to fuel flow and engine speed; convective dissipation is driven by airspeed and air density:
`rpm_norm = max(1000.0, RPM) / 5000.0`  
`cooling_mass_flow = Density_Ratio * (max(20.0, TAS_KNOTS) / 85.0)`  
`heat_generated_index = (fuel_flow_expected / 18.5) * (rpm_norm ^ 1.1)`  
`heat_dissipation_factor = max(0.5, 0.75 * cooling_mass_flow + 0.25)`

Baseline CHT:
`base_CHT = OAT_C + 75.0 + (35.0 * heat_generated_index / heat_dissipation_factor)`  
`CHT_expected = max(50.0, min(130.0, base_CHT)) [°C]`

Individual cylinder geometric distribution offsets (Rotax rear cylinders run slightly warmer):
* `CHT_1 = CHT_expected - 1.0 °C`
* `CHT_2 = CHT_expected + 1.5 °C`
* `CHT_3 = CHT_expected - 1.5 °C`
* `CHT_4 = CHT_expected + 1.0 °C`

#### 5. Exhaust Gas Temperature (EGT)
Combustion temperature peaks at stoichiometric conditions and drops with throttle-dependent enrichment:
`base_EGT = 760.0 + 35.0 * sin(throttle_norm * pi) + (OAT_C * 0.15) [°C]`
* `EGT_1 = base_EGT - 3.0 °C`
* `EGT_2 = base_EGT + 2.0 °C`
* `EGT_3 = base_EGT - 1.0 °C`
* `EGT_4 = base_EGT + 2.0 °C`

#### 6. Lubrication Oil Pressure & Temperature
`oil_temp_expected = max(55.0, min(115.0, OAT_C + 60.0 + (28.0 * heat_generated_index / heat_dissipation_factor))) [°C]`  
`oil_press_expected = max(2.0, min(5.0, 2.2 + (2.3 * rpm_norm) - (0.015 * (oil_temp_expected - 80.0)))) [bar]`

#### 7. Electrical & Mechanical Outputs
* `bus_voltage_expected = 14.10 V`
* `battery_current_expected = 3.5 + 2.0 * (1.0 - throttle_norm) A`
* `vib_gearbox_expected = 0.35 + 0.35 * (rpm_norm ^ 2.0) mm/s`

## 4. Residual Formulation, Sensor Sanity & Anomaly Indexing

### 4.1 Residual Vector Generation
For each 20 Hz frame, the system computes the difference between actual readings and theoretical expectations across 14 channels:
`Delta_Signal = Actual_Signal - Expected_Signal`

Each residual is normalized by its standard nominal flight tolerance divisor:
* `z_CHT_1 = abs(Delta_CHT_1) / 4.0`
* `z_CHT_2 = abs(Delta_CHT_2) / 4.0`
* `z_CHT_3 = abs(Delta_CHT_3) / 4.0`
* `z_CHT_4 = abs(Delta_CHT_4) / 4.0`
* `z_EGT_1 = abs(Delta_EGT_1) / 15.0`
* `z_EGT_2 = abs(Delta_EGT_2) / 15.0`
* `z_EGT_3 = abs(Delta_EGT_3) / 15.0`
* `z_EGT_4 = abs(Delta_EGT_4) / 15.0`
* `z_OilPress = abs(Delta_OilPress) / 0.30`
* `z_OilTemp = abs(Delta_OilTemp) / 5.0`
* `z_FuelFlow = abs(Delta_FuelFlow) / 1.5`
* `z_MAP = abs(Delta_MAP) / 3.0`
* `z_VibRMS = abs(Delta_VibRMS) / 0.25`
* `z_BusVoltage = abs(Delta_BusVoltage) / 0.35`

The composite anomaly score blends peak single-channel stress with root-mean-square whole-engine stress:
`z_rms = sqrt((sum of z_i ^ 2) / 14)`  
`z_max = max(z_1, z_2, ..., z_14)`  
`z_composite = 0.65 * z_max + 0.35 * z_rms`  
`Anomaly_Score = 1.0 - exp(-0.45 * z_composite)` (bounded between 0.0 and 1.0)

```
ANOMALY SCORE REGIME:
[ 0.00 ──────── 0.35 )   ──► NOMINAL (Engine operating within physics baseline)
[ 0.35 ──────── 0.65 )   ──► EARLY DRIFT (Sub-threshold micro-trending active)
[ 0.65 ──────── 1.00 ]   ──► FAULT / CRITICAL ANOMALY (Majority voting gate triggered)
```

### 4.2 Sensor Sanity & Instrumentation Protection
Before residuals are passed to prognostics or classifiers, `SensorSanityValidator` (`backend/physics/sensor_validator.py`) runs three validation checks:

```
                                INCOMING RAW SENSOR READING
                                             │
                      ┌──────────────────────┴──────────────────────┐
                      ▼                                             ▼
           PHYSICAL THERMAL RAMP                       ELECTRICAL SENSOR ARTIFACT
      • dT/dt <= 1.5 °C/s                         • dT/dt > 10.0 °C/s (Unphysical)
      • Correlated with adjacent CHT & Oil        • Isolated single-channel jump
      • Natural analog ripple +-0.2 °C            • Zero variance = Frozen ADC buffer
      ──────────────────────────────              ───────────────────────────────────
      ==> TRUE ENGINE OVERHEAT FAULT              ==> SENSOR FAULT / OPEN CIRCUIT
```

1. **Rate of Change Limit (|Delta x / Delta t|):**
   * Physical CHT rate limit: 1.5 °C/s.
   * If Delta CHT > 10.0 °C in a single 50 ms frame (equivalent to 200 °C/s), the channel is flagged as `RATE_SPIKE` (thermocouple open-circuit).
2. **Frozen ADC Detection:**
   * Over a rolling 100-frame window (5 seconds at 20 Hz), natural analog jitter variance must exceed 0.01 (°C)^2.
   * Zero variance flags `FROZEN_ADC` (stuck bus/converter).
3. **Cross-Sensor Correlation:**
   * True combustion thermal runaway on Cylinder #2 produces correlated rises in oil return temperature (dOil/dt > 0.02 °C/s) and exhaust temperature (dEGT_2/dt > 0.20 °C/s). An isolated jump without correlated movement is reclassified as `ISOLATED_SPIKE`.

## 5. Prognostics Engine: Degradation Trending, Monte Carlo RUL & Go/No-Go Decision Matrix

The pre-flight certification decision in Pillar 1 is computed by `ProbabilisticRULEstimator` executing inside the background `PrognosticsWorker` (`backend/ml/trend_analyser.py`).

```
                    PROGNOSTICS & GO / NO-GO PIPELINE ARCHITECTURE
┌─────────────────────────┐
│ 20 Hz Main Engine Loop  │ ──► Appends (t, Score, Channel_Scores) to ScoreBuffer (maxlen=3600)
└─────────────────────────┘
             │
             ▼ (Every 20 Seconds in Daemon Worker Thread)
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ DegradationTrendAnalyser.analyse()                                                     │
│ • Evaluates 3 sliding windows: Short (2 min), Medium (10 min), Long (30 min)           │
│ • Fits candidate degradation equations: Linear, Exponential, Power-Law                 │
│ • Selects best model using Akaike Information Criterion: AIC = n*ln(SSE/n) + 2k        │
│ • Computes drift rate (units/min) and analytical time-to-breach                        │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
             │
             ▼
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ ProbabilisticRULEstimator.estimate()                                                   │
│ • Runs 500-sample Monte Carlo perturbation on model parameters (a, b)                  │
│ • Parameter uncertainty: sigma_b = (1.0 - R^2) / sqrt(n) * 2.0                         │
│ • Samples distribution of time-to-failure ──► Sorts samples ──► Extracts p10, p50, p90 │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
             │
             ▼
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ ProbabilisticRULEstimator.go_no_go(planned_hours=18.0, safety_margin=2.0)              │
│ • If planned_hours > worst_component_RUL_p10              ──► NO-GO (Pre-emptive Maint)│
│ • If planned_hours > worst_component_RUL_p10 - safety_margin ──► CAUTION (Narrow Margin) │
│ • If worst_component_RUL_p10 >= planned_hours + safety_margin ──► GO (Certified)        │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 5.1 Multi-Model Curve Fitting & AIC Selection
For each subsystem channel, the analyzer fits three candidate degradation models across three time windows (2 min, 10 min, 30 min):
1. **Linear Model:** `y(t) = a + b * t` fitted via closed-form Ordinary Least Squares (OLS).
2. **Exponential Model:** `y(t) = a * exp(b * t)` via log-transform: `ln(y) = ln(a) + b * t`.
3. **Power-Law Model:** `y(t) = a * (t ^ b)` via log-transform: `ln(y) = ln(a) + b * ln(t)`.

The optimal model is selected by minimizing the Akaike Information Criterion (AIC):
`AIC = n * ln(SSE / n) + 2 * k`  
Where `n` is the number of historical points, `SSE` is the sum of squared errors, and `k` is the parameter count (`k = 2`).

### 5.2 500-Iteration Monte Carlo Uncertainty Sampling
Rather than relying on a deterministic point estimate, parameter uncertainty is modeled from the fit quality:
`sigma_b = ((1.0 - R^2) / sqrt(n)) * 2.0`

For each of the 500 Monte Carlo iterations:
* `b_perturbed = model.b * (1.0 + Gaussian_Noise(mean=0, std=sigma_b))`
* `a_perturbed = model.a * (1.0 + Gaussian_Noise(mean=0, std=sigma_b * 0.3))`
* A binary search calculates the time-to-breach where `y(t_breach) >= Fault_Threshold_Score`.

Sorting the 500 simulated breach times yields the statistical percentiles:
* **p10 (Conservative Bound):** 10th percentile — 90% confidence that the component will survive at least this long. **This is the authoritative metric used for pre-flight Go/No-Go certification.**
* **p50 (Median Bound):** 50th percentile nominal expected remaining life.
* **p90 (Optimistic Bound):** 90th percentile upper limit.

### 5.3 Go/No-Go Decision Rules
Let `T_planned` be the planned sortie flight hours (e.g., 18.0 hrs) and `Safety_Margin = 2.0 hrs`:
* **NO-GO / PRE-EMPTIVE MAINTENANCE REQUIRED:**  
  Triggered if `T_planned > worst_component_RUL_p10`.
* **CAUTION / NARROW SAFETY MARGIN:**  
  Triggered if `T_planned > worst_component_RUL_p10 - Safety_Margin`.
* **GO / PROPULSION SYSTEM CERTIFIED:**  
  Triggered if `worst_component_RUL_p10 >= T_planned + Safety_Margin`.

## 8. Critical Engineering Audit: Gaps, Simplifications & Refinement Roadmap

To ensure technical credibility when presenting to aerospace engineers and DRDO evaluators, the following engineering nuances and upgrade steps are documented:

### 1. Inactive Autoencoder Weights
* **Current Behavior:** In `detection_pipeline.py`, `self._autoencoder.load()` looks for `rotax_autoencoder.json` under `backend/ml/models/`. Because only the Random Forest model exists on disk, `is_trained` remains `False`, and `ae_score` defaults to `0.0`.
* **Operational Effect:** Anomaly detection relies on the physics-based normalized Z-score composite equation rather than a fused Autoencoder + Z-score.
* **Upgrade:** Run `scripts/train_ml_models.py` to train and serialize the 14-8-4-8-14 autoencoder weights to disk.

### 2. Vibration Spectral Nyquist Limit at 20 Hz
* **Current Behavior:** At a 20 Hz telemetry rate, the Nyquist frequency is 10 Hz. The 3rd harmonic of the propeller reduction shaft at 5,000 RPM is approximately 103 Hz, which exceeds the 10 Hz Nyquist ceiling.
* **Implementation:** `spectral_analyser.py` explicitly handles this via `use_rms_fallback = True`, tracking windowed RMS energy envelopes rather than a discrete Goertzel DFT filter.
* **Upgrade for Flight Hardware:** When integrating physical accelerometer hardware, stream high-frequency vibration data over a dedicated 1 kHz to 5 kHz edge buffer.

### 3. Cross-Sensor Correlation Asymmetry
* **Current Behavior:** In `sensor_validator.py`, the cross-channel thermal correlation check is specifically implemented for `CHT_2` against `OIL_TEMP` and `EGT_2`.
* **Upgrade:** Generalize the cross-sensor correlation matrix to evaluate all 4 cylinders symmetrically (correlating each `CHT_i` with `EGT_i`, oil return temperature, and adjacent cylinder heads).

### 4. Tri-Source Dataset Fusion Gap
* **Current Behavior:** `dataset_fusion_engine.py` instantiates `NASABenchmarkParser`, but `build_fused_master_datasets()` merges Garmin G3X flight logs (Source 1) and DRDO synthetic mission logs (Source 3), while NASA C-MAPSS files (Source 2) are counted in the manifest but not feature-merged.
* **Upgrade:** Connect the parsed run-to-failure bearing vibration records from NASA C-MAPSS into the vibration RMS feature columns of the master training dataset.
