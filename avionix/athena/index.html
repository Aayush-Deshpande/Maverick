Athena

The intelligence and data foundation of USAvionix, built to understand the environment, extract meaning, and continuously learn.

Athena is not a single model. It is a collection of models, data systems, and learning processes that work together to interpret the world and continuously improve, determining what something is and what it means in context.

Perception & Reasoning

Vision and language models detect, track, and interpret intent, risk, and behavior with context.

Structured Intelligence

Normalizes raw sensor data into detections, tracks, and inferred events for real-time use by Phalanx.

Continuous Learning

The platform's long-term memory. Data from every flight flows back to refine and retrain the models, an advantage that compounds with every deployment.
