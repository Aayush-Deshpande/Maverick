## 3. Factual Classification: What is Operational vs. Simulated vs. Limited

### 3.1 Genuinely Operational & Functional
1. **Thread-Safe Historical Data Ingestion:**
   * `ScoreBuffer` reliably receives 20 Hz multi-channel frames from `DetectionPipeline` without blocking the real-time loop.
   * Lock acquisition during snapshotting takes $< 0.5\text{ ms}$, ensuring zero frame drops.
2. **First-Principles Trend Regression Math:**
   * All three regression algorithms (Linear OLS, Exponential log-linear, Power-Law log-log) are implemented in pure Python with exact closed-form equations.
   * Model selection via AIC correctly identifies the winning curve based on true residual sum of squares.
3. **500-Sample Monte Carlo Empirical Uncertainty:**
   * `ProbabilisticRULEstimator` generates genuine non-parametric empirical distributions of failure time, deriving valid $p_{10}$ conservative bounds.
4. **WebSocket & Dashboard Integration:**
   * The backend broadcasts `early_warning_trend` over `/ws/telemetry`, and the React frontend dynamically renders the early warning card.

---

### 3.2 What is Simulated / Synthetic
1. **Telemetry Source:**
   * In the current prototype, telemetry is synthesized by `TelemetryStreamer` (`backend/telemetry/can_streamer.py`) or `RotaxTimeSeriesGenerator` (`backend/telemetry/rotax_dataset_generator.py`).
   * The physics, atmosphere (Ladakh/Thar), first-order differential lags ($\tau_{\text{CHT}} = 6.5\text{ s}$, $\tau_{\text{Oil}} = 35\text{ s}$), and Gaussian sensor noise are mathematically rigorous, but originate from synthetic models rather than physical hardware CAN bus transceivers.
2. **Fault Injection Mechanism:**
   * Faults and micro-drifts are injected via software ramps ($t_{\text{ramp}} = 8.0\text{ s}$) in `TelemetryStreamer.set_fault()`.

---

### 3.3 Identified Technical Gaps & Engineering Limitations

#### Gap 1: Buffer Capacity vs. 30-Minute Window Slicing
* **Evidence:** `engine_service.py` allocates `self.score_buffer = ScoreBuffer(maxlen=7200)`. At 20 Hz, $7,200\text{ frames} = 360.0\text{ seconds} = 6.0\text{ minutes}$.
* **Impact:** In `DegradationTrendAnalyser.analyse()`, the `long` window is configured for $1,800\text{ s}$ ($30\text{ min}$). Because the circular buffer only retains 6 minutes of uncompressed data, the 30-minute window will only ever evaluate up to 6 minutes of history during continuous operation.
* **Severity:** Medium (Prognostics still function accurately over the 2-minute and 6-minute medium windows, but the 30-minute slow-drift window is truncated).
* **Fix Required:** Implement a downsampled historical ring buffer (e.g. 1 Hz downsampled buffer storing 1,800 points for the 30-minute window).

#### Gap 2: Asymmetric Component Mapping
* **Evidence:** In `trend_analyser.py`, `COMPONENT_CHANNELS` maps `Cylinder_Head_Assembly` specifically to `d_CHT_2` and `Ignition_Harness` to `d_EGT_2`.
* **Impact:** Thermal micro-drifts occurring exclusively on Cylinder #1, #3, or #4 will be captured by the whole-engine `__composite__` channel, but will not be attributed to `Cylinder_Head_Assembly` specifically.
* **Severity:** Low (System still detects the anomaly and alerts GCS, but component attribution is asymmetric).
* **Fix Required:** Generalize `COMPONENT_CHANNELS` to scan `max(d_CHT_1..4)` and `max(d_EGT_1..4)`.
