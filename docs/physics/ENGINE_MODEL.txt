1.1 backend/sim/atmosphere.py
------------------------------------------------------------
ISA atmosphere with ISA deviation and humidity (card M1-02, R 21.4, P Part 4 Step 1.3a). Valid below 11 km.

1.2 backend/sim/induction.py
------------------------------------------------------------
Induction: air filter -> throttle (compressible orifice) -> manifold filling -> cylinders (card M1-04, R 21.4).

  dMAP/dt = (R T_m / V_m) * (mdot_throttle - mdot_cyl)
  mdot_cyl = eta_v * MAP / (R T_m) * V_d * rpm / 120                     (4-stroke speed-density)
  mdot_throttle = Cd A(theta) p_up / sqrt(R T_up) * Psi(MAP / p_up)       (isentropic orifice, choked below p*)
  p_up = p_source - k_filter * filter_factor * mdot^2                     (F13 raises filter_factor)

The manifold is faster than the 0.05 s plant step, so each step solves the quasi-steady MAP (flow balance) and moves
toward it with the exact exponential of the linearised filling time constant: stable at any step, physical lag.
The real rpm is an input (removes the old fixed 5200 rpm, audit V5). For turbo engines p_source is the charge
pressure from backend/sim/turbo.py (M1-10); for naturally aspirated engines it is ambient pressure.

1.3 backend/sim/turbo.py
------------------------------------------------------------
Turbocharger + boost control + intercooler, quasi-steady with spool lag (card M1-10, R 21.4, P 1.3j).

Boost control (TCU wastegate or VGT) holds the charge pressure that gives the configured MAP target at wide-open
throttle. What the turbo can deliver is limited by its maximum shaft speed: on the top speed line the available
pressure ratio falls as corrected mass flow rises,
  PR_max(m_corr) = a - b * m_corr,     m_corr = mdot * sqrt(T_in / T_ref) / (p_in / p_ref)
so the critical altitude EMERGES (wastegate fully closed and MAP still falling). The line is CALIBRATED flat (b = 0)
from the documented critical altitude of the turbo's own capability. Where the manufacturer documents a LOWER
take-off critical altitude (an engine whose config gives take-off power to a lower altitude than continuous power),
that limit is the boost controller's: the TCU caps the take-off pressure ratio (takeoff_pr_limit, CALIBRATED), since a
single compressor speed line cannot give more pressure ratio at the higher corrected flow of the 16,000 ft point.
Faults act on parameters: boost leak (F14) loses a share of the boost and the controller compensates until PR_max;
wastegate/VGT stuck open (F15) leaves no boost; stuck closed (F16) delivers PR_max (overboost); bearing wear (F17)
cuts the PR margin and slows the spool; intercooler fouling (F18) cuts its effectiveness.

1.4 backend/sim/fuel.py
------------------------------------------------------------
Fuel systems (card M1-11, R 21.4, P 1.3l): port injection, carburettor and common rail; each
engine's configs/engines fuel_system picks one.

Faults act on parameters only:
  injector flow coefficient per cylinder   (F05 clog, F06 coking, F07 stuck open, F08 stuck closed)
  HP pump / rail health                    (F09 rail pressure decay: rail pressure falls, the ECU stretches the pulse
                                            width up to its limit, then delivery falls)
  low-pressure supply health               (F10: supply pressure falls; port injectors run lean, CI rail sags)
Fuel flow published by the engine is the sum of what each cylinder actually received (no constant offsets).

1.5 backend/sim/combustion.py
------------------------------------------------------------
Per-cylinder combustion (card M1-05, R 21.4, P Part 4 Step 1.3d).

  W_indicated = m_fuel_burnt * LHV * eta_i * f_lambda * f_timing * quality
SI rich (lambda < 1): only the oxygen-limited fuel burns (m_burnt = lambda * m_fuel). SI lean: efficiency falls past
the lean optimum and the charge misfires beyond the lean limit. CI: delivered fuel is capped by the smoke limit
(lambda_min); CI is always lean so f_lambda = 1. eta_i is the reference indicated efficiency, CALIBRATED in M1-06 so
each engine meets its take-off power. COV_IMEP and the knock index are published as derived channels.
Faults act here only through inputs: quality (F01/F08 misfire, F02 instability), timing error (F11), ignition energy
(F04 lane loss), fuel per cylinder (F05-F07, from fuel.py in M1-11).

1.6 backend/sim/engine.py
------------------------------------------------------------
Mean-value engine assembly (cards M1-06 onward; grows through M1-07..M1-14, R 21.2-21.5).

Steady-state power chain used for calibration and the T-PHY tests:
  induction (MAP, air flow) -> fuel per cylinder (lambda) -> combustion (indicated work) -> minus friction (FMEP)
  -> brake power. Calibration (R 21.5 step 1): eta_i is solved so brake power at sea level ISA, wide-open throttle and
  rated rpm equals the take-off power in configs/engines (status CALIBRATED, computed at start-up, not stored).

1.7 backend/sim/rotor.py
------------------------------------------------------------
Crankshaft + gearbox + propeller torque balance with a constant-speed governor (card M1-03, R 21.4, P 1.3b).

  J * domega/dt = Q_engine - Q_prop / G
  P_prop = Cp * rho * n^3 * D^5,  n = propeller rev/s,  J_adv = V / (n D)
  Cp = cp_scale * pitch * max(0, 1 - J_adv / J0)          (generic curve, JSBSim FGPropeller form)
Fixed pitch: pitch = 1. Constant speed: a PI governor moves pitch between its stops to hold the rpm setpoint.
cp_scale is calibrated so the propeller absorbs the engine's take-off power at its rated propeller speed at sea level
and the design airspeed (CALIBRATED), so only the installation data in configs/installations is ASSUMED.

1.8 backend/sim/thermal.py
------------------------------------------------------------
Heads, exhaust, coolant loop, oil system and gearbox as lumped heat balances (card M1-07, R 21.4).

Per cylinder i (fuel energy Q_i = mdot_f,i * LHV, burnt share q_i):
  C_h dCHT_i/dt = f_wall q_i Q_i - G_hc (CHT_i - T_c) - G_ha s_air eff_i (CHT_i - T_amb)
  EGT_i = T_man + (q_i Q_i - W_i - f_wall q_i Q_i) / (mdot_exh,i cp_exh), first-order lag
Coolant: C_c dT_c/dt = sum G_hc (CHT_i - T_c) - G_rad s_air rad flow (T_c - T_amb)
Oil:     C_o dT_o/dt = s_f P_friction + f_oil sum q_i Q_i - G_oil s_air cooler (T_o - T_amb)
         p_oil = min(p_relief, k * rpm * pump_health * mu(T_o) / mu_ref)   (Vogel viscosity)
         below the minimum pressure the bearing friction rises (boundary lubrication): the seizure chain
Gearbox: C_g dT_g/dt = (1 - eta_gear) P_brake - G_g s_air (T_g - T_amb)
s_air = (rho V / rho_cal V_cal)^0.8 scales every air-side conductance (hot day, altitude, slow flight run hotter).
Conductances and capacities are CALIBRATED once per engine (R 21.5 step 4): at max continuous power, ISA+15 and the
design airspeed the temperatures sit at the targets in configs/physics; time constants come from the same file.
Faults act only through the health inputs (F19 cooling_eff, F20 coolant_flow, F21 radiator, F23 pump_health,
F25 oil_cooler, F27 gear_loss).

1.9 backend/sim/electrical.py
------------------------------------------------------------
Electrical system: alternators, regulator, battery state of charge, bus loads (card M1-08, R 21.4, P 1.3h).

  alternator current available = I_rated * clamp((rpm - cut_in) / (full - cut_in), 0, 1) * health      (F32)
  supply covers load + charge  -> bus = regulator setpoint (+ F33 offset); battery charges (limited)
  supply short of load         -> battery supplies the rest; bus = OCV(SOC) - I_batt * R_int / health   (F34)
  SOC integrates the battery current over its capacity (capacity also scales with battery health).
Installation data comes from configs/installations/electrical.json (per engine id, else 'default').

1.10 backend/sim/vibration.py
------------------------------------------------------------
Vibration orders (card M1-09, R 21.4, P 1.3i): velocity amplitudes in mm/s at the gearbox sensor.

Order m of the crankshaft torque (per crank revolution) for cylinders firing at theta_k = k * 720 / n deg:
  A_m = | sum_k T_k exp(-i m theta_k) | / n,   T_k = per-cylinder torque fraction (firing sequence order)
m = n/2 is the firing order (proportional to mean torque); m = 0.5 and 1 appear only with imbalance (misfire,
injector faults, compression loss), on top of a small manufacturing spread, and are amplified by the mount
transmissibility at low orders (low_order_gain). Propeller 1x (F28), gear mesh (F27)
and the mount transmissibility (F29) are separate terms. RMS = sqrt(sum of squares).

1.11 backend/sim/plant.py
------------------------------------------------------------
The dynamic engine plant: one fixed step of the whole engine (card M1-12, R 21.3, R 21.4 state machine).

Order per step (R 21.3): atmosphere -> ECU (timing, limiters, lanes) -> quasi-steady power chain at the CURRENT rpm
(induction/turbo/fuel/combustion/friction, backend/sim/engine.py) -> torque lag -> rotor (rpm from the torque balance)
-> thermal/coolant/oil/gearbox -> electrical -> vibration -> state machine. Faults arrive ONLY as parameter changes in
`health` (backend/sim/faults.py, M1-13); nothing here edits an output.

Published engine_state is what an observer can see: OFF, CRANKING, RUNNING, FAILED. "Degraded" is a verdict of the
status evaluator from evidence (M2-01), never a plant flag (that would leak the injected fault).

1.12 backend/sim/faults.py
------------------------------------------------------------
Registry faults -> plant parameters (card M1-13, R 21.6, P Part 0 D3).

Every active fault contributes its registry `parameter_effects` (configs/faults.json) at its effective severity
  s(t) = severity * min(1, (t - t0) / ramp_s)        (simulated seconds; D8)
  scale: factor = 1 + (v - 1) s        add: delta = v s        set: value = default + (v - default) s
The result is the `health` dict that backend/sim/plant.py consumes. Faults NEVER edit an output: readings move only
because the equations run with these parameters. The vocabulary on the left (component.parameter) is the registry's;
the mapping below connects it to the model's inputs. Sensor faults (S01-S07) are returned separately for the sensor
layer (backend/sim/sensors.py): the engine itself is healthy.

1.13 backend/sim/sensors.py
------------------------------------------------------------
Sensor layer (card M1-13, R 21.2 sensors.py, S01-S07). The engine is healthy in a sensor fault: only the
measurement changes, so related channels do not move (that is how it is told apart from a process fault).
Each channel has its own seeded random stream (R 3.12) so a fault on one sensor never changes another's noise.
  S01 freeze: holds the last good value      S02 bias: constant offset       S03 drift: offset grows with time
  S04 noise: sigma x gain                    S05 dropout: no value (None)     S06 out of range: impossible value
  S07 lane split: lane B disagrees with lane A (published as <channel>.lane_b)

1.14 backend/sim/source.py
------------------------------------------------------------
The live plant behind the PlantSource/Frame contract (card M1-14, P Step 1.4).

SimSource runs backend/sim (SimEngine + registry fault parameters + sensor layer) and emits (Frame, TruthRecord)
pairs, so the runtime, detectors and API keep their contract while the physics underneath is the M1 model.

Faults are registry faults (configs/faults.json ids). Each keeps its own t0, so adding or clearing one fault never
restarts another's ramp. The Frame carries only measured values (sensor layer applied); the TruthRecord carries the
active faults and plant state and goes to evaluation code only.

1.15 backend/sim/maps.py
------------------------------------------------------------
Performance maps from the calibrated plant (card M1-14, P Step 1.3q).

The map is a sweep of the steady chain (backend/sim/engine.py) over rpm x load at ISA sea level: spark ignition sweeps
the throttle (MAP follows), a quality-governed diesel sweeps the power lever. Brake power, BSFC, brake efficiency,
MAP and mean EGT are outputs of the model, never configured. Written once per engine to var/maps/<engine_id>.json and
served by /api/v1/engines/{engine_id}/maps; the engineer view plots the live operating point on it.
