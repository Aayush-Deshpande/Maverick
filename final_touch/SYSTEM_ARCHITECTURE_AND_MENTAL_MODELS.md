### Mental Model 1: The Truth vs. Observation Duality (`VirtualEngine` vs. `Frame` vs. `TruthRecord`)

In flawed digital twin architectures, the "simulated sensor values" are generated by taking the twin's own internal mathematical equations, adding Gaussian white noise, and adding an artificial offset when a fault is toggled. When the digital twin evaluates the incoming telemetry, it compares the data against the exact same equations that created it. Under such an arrangement, the residual is always identically zero (plus noise), any simple threshold detects the fault, and reported accuracy figures are completely deceptive.

**In ANUMAAN, this problem is solved by establishing an unbreachable wall between Plant and Twin:**

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    subgraph Plant["Physical Reality / VirtualEngine (backend/plant/)"]
        direction TB
        EVar["Engine Variation<br/>(Friction, Heat Scale, Volumetric Eff)"]
        SensModel["Sensor Dynamics<br/>(Bias Drift, Thermal Lag, Quantization)"]
        FaultInj["Hidden Fault Injection<br/>(Coking, Needle Stick, Oil Leak)"]
        PhysPlant["Higher-Fidelity Physics<br/>(Crank Dynamics, Turbo, Non-linear Oil)"]
        EVar --> PhysPlant
        SensModel --> PhysPlant
        FaultInj --> PhysPlant
    end

    subgraph Seam["The Unbreachable Architectural Seam"]
        Frame["Frame (backend/core/frame.py)<br/>PUBLIC TELEMETRY ONLY<br/>(RPM, MAP, CHT, EGT, Oil P, Volts)<br/>⚠️ Zero Fault Labels<br/>⚠️ Zero Health Indices<br/>⚠️ Zero RUL Values"]
        Truth["TruthRecord<br/>ISOLATED EVALUATION HARNESS<br/>(Ground Truth Fault ID, Active Severity,<br/>True Wear Parameters, Failure Time)"]
    end

    subgraph Twin["Digital Twin Intelligence (backend/detect/ & ml/)"]
        direction TB
        Resid["Residual Detector (Tier 0)<br/>Frame - Expected Baseline"]
        Classif["Classifier / Reservoir (Tier 1)<br/>Multi-channel Residual Pattern"]
    end

    PhysPlant -->|Produces| Frame
    PhysPlant -->|Evaluator Only| Truth
    Frame -->|Input to| Resid
    Resid -->|Triggers| Classif
    Truth -.->|Used ONLY in offline benchmark tests| Benchmark["Evaluation Harness<br/>(backend/evaluation/)"]
```

#### Key Architectural Invariants:
1. **Model Mismatch is Real:** Because `VirtualEngine` includes manufacturing tolerances, thermal inertia, and sensor lag that the twin's observer model does not know, the nominal residuals are **non-zero and structured**.
2. **Zero Label Leakage:** The `Frame` contract strictly forbids passing `FAULT_ID`, `HEALTH_INDEX`, or `RUL_HOURS`. The detector must infer anomalies purely from sensor residuals without prior knowledge.
3. **`TruthRecord` Isolation:** The ground truth is written to a distinct stream accessible solely by the automated test suite in `tests/` and benchmarks in `backend/evaluation/`.

### Mental Model 3: The OSA-CBM / ISO 13374 Six-Layer Architecture

ANUMAAN is architected directly against the international standard for Condition-Based Maintenance (**ISO 13374 / OSA-CBM**), mapped across six logical processing layers (`backend/osacbm.py`):

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    L1["Layer 1: Data Acquisition (DA)<br/>• Physical/Virtual CAN-bus Ingest (20 Hz)<br/>• High-rate Crank Tooth Sensors (≤ 25 ns timestamps)<br/>• Sensor Quality Bitmasks (stale, frozen, out-of-range)"]
    
    L2["Layer 2: Data Manipulation (DM)<br/>• Thermodynamic density derating (ICAO Standard Atmosphere)<br/>• Crank-angle domain transformation (0° to 720° cycle)<br/>• FFT Vibration Decomposition & Spectral Harmonics"]
    
    L3["Layer 3: State Detection (SD)<br/>• 13-feature Tail Calibration Residuals (TailCalibration.z)<br/>• FlyBloom LSH novelty scoring & Mahalanobis distance<br/>• Persistence Gating (3 of last 5 frames above conformal threshold)"]
    
    L4["Layer 4: Health Assessment (HA)<br/>• Multi-class Fault Classifier (DRDO 8 fault categories)<br/>• Inter-cylinder thermal & combustion imbalance metrics<br/>• Root-cause component localization (Mesh Locator)"]
    
    L5["Layer 5: Prognostic Assessment (PA)<br/>• Split-Conformal RUL prediction with 95% statistical coverage<br/>• Arrhenius thermal degradation & Miner's cumulative fatigue<br/>• Computed Mission Completion Reliability (Rm)"]
    
    L6["Layer 6: Advisory Generation (AG)<br/>• Deterministic ATA-Chapter Directives (ATA 72-79)<br/>• 4-Step Pilot Emergency Procedures<br/>• Depot-level Ground Crew Maintenance Work Orders"]

    L1 --> L2 --> L3 --> L4 --> L5 --> L6
```

### Mental Model 5: Telemetry Rate Dichotomy (Scalar 20 Hz vs. High-Rate Waveform DSP)

Engine physical degradation manifests across two distinct temporal scales. A diagnostic system that only reads scalar telemetry is blind to combustion dynamics, while a system that only processes waveforms drowns in computational complexity:

```mermaid
graph LR
    subgraph LowRate["Low-Rate Domain (20 Hz / 50 ms) — Frame"]
        direction TB
        Scalars["Scalar Telemetry:<br/>• CHT (4 cylinders)<br/>• EGT (4 cylinders)<br/>• Oil Pressure & Temperature<br/>• Fuel Rail Pressure & Mass Flow<br/>• Bus Voltage & Battery Current<br/>• Manifold Absolute Pressure (MAP)"]
        LRObs["Observable Failure Modes:<br/>• Severe Cooling Blockage (CHT runaway)<br/>• Total Oil Pressure Loss<br/>• Turbocharger Wastegate Stuck Open<br/>• Alternator Belt Failure / Voltage Sag<br/>• Sensor Bias Drift & Stuck Readings"]
        Scalars --> LRObs
    end

    subgraph HighRate["High-Rate Domain (10 kHz - 100 kHz) — CycleBlock"]
        direction TB
        Waveforms["Waveform Dynamics:<br/>• Crankshaft 60-2 tooth timestamps (≤ 25 ns)<br/>• In-cylinder pressure transients<br/>• Gearbox accelerometer vibration (kHz)<br/>• Fuel rail acoustic pressure pulses<br/>• Alternator bridge-rectifier ripple"]
        HRObs["Observable Failure Modes:<br/>• Early Injector Coking & Partial Orifice Clog<br/>• Needle Valve Bounce & Seat Sticking<br/>• Incipient Knock / Detonation<br/>• Indicated Mean Effective Pressure (IMEP) Imbalance<br/>• Planetary Gearbox Tooth Pitting & Bearing Fatigue"]
        Waveforms --> HRObs
    end
```
