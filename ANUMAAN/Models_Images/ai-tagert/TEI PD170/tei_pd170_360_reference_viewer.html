Component & Fault Highlights 11 Subsystems

Reduction Gearbox & Prop Hub

Conical aluminum casing with stiffening ribs, 6-bolt propeller hub, sight glass, drain plug, and safety wiring.

⚠️ Fault: Gearbox oil pressure drop / Flange bearing wear
Hydraulic Propeller Governor

Single-lever hydromechanical prop governor mounted lower right on gearbox with steel hydraulic hard lines.

⚠️ Fault: Pitch control failure / Hydraulic seal leak
Two-Stage Sequential Turbochargers

High-Pressure (HP) and Low-Pressure (LP) turbos with cast crossover exhaust duct and oil feed/return lines.

⚠️ Fault: Turbo overboost / Compressor stall / Oil seal leak
Dual Wastegate Actuators

Black primary pneumatic actuator + Red billet anodized TiAL-style actuator with stainless linkage rod and jam nuts.

⚠️ Fault: Wastegate flap sticking / Boost loss at altitude
Exhaust Downpipe & Heat Shield

Stainless steel downpipe with V-band clamp, dimpled metallic thermal insulation blanket over exhaust manifold.

⚠️ Fault: Exhaust manifold crack / V-band clamp loose
Dual 4.5 kW 28V DC Alternators

Vertically stacked gold/yellow rotor housings with perimeter cooling slots and heavy gauge terminal cables.

⚠️ Fault: Alternator 1/2 diode short / Voltage regulator trip
Common Rail Fuel Rail & 4 Hard Lines

Yellow support rail bracket, 4 stainless injector lines feeding high-pressure solenoid injectors with vibration dampeners.

⚠️ Fault: Injection pressure drop / Injector #3 solenoid fault
Oil & Fuel Conditioning (ASAS & Cooler)

ASAS cylindrical diesel filter canister, horizontal spin-on oil filter, finned billet oil cooler heat exchanger box.

⚠️ Fault: Fuel filter clog / Oil cooler bypass valve open
Flywheel & Ring Gear Assembly

Precision machined steel flywheel with starter ring gear, bell housing rim with alignment dowels, rear coolant neck.

⚠️ Fault: Ring gear tooth damage / Starter motor failure
Aerospace Mil-Spec Wiring Harness

Expandable black braided loom, yellow heat-shrink identification sleeves, Mil-Spec P-clamps, dual FADEC interface.

⚠️ Fault: Sensor ground intermittent / CAN bus communication error
