## 1. Executive Problem Statement Breakdown

### 1.1 The Operational Problem (Physical & Tactical Reality)
A Medium Altitude Long Endurance (MALE) UAV operates on an **18 to 24-hour continuous sortie** at altitudes between **15,000 and 30,000 feet** driven by a **single piston engine**. Unlike commercial airliners or twin-engine military aircraft, there is **zero engine redundancy**. If the engine experiences catastrophic failure (thermal seizure, scavenge oil starvation, crankshaft fracture, or uncontained valvetrain failure) during a deep-penetration Intelligence, Surveillance, Target Acquisition, and Reconnaissance (ISTAR) mission, the aircraft cannot maintain glide slope over mountainous terrain (e.g., Eastern Ladakh, Siachen) or desert borders (e.g., Thar), leading to total asset destruction and loss of strategic reconnaissance payloads.

In crewed general aviation, a pilot continuously senses the engine:
* Detecting acoustic timbre shifts (gearbox chatter, valve lash drift)
* Smelling volatilized oil vapors from crankcase breathers
* Feeling high-frequency airframe buzz through pedals and yoke
* Observing subtle, correlated cross-needle creep (e.g., CHT rising 0.4°C/min while oil pressure decays 0.1 bar/hr) long before limits are breached.

In a MALE UAV controlled from a Ground Control Station (GCS) 250 to 1,000 km away over a satellite link (SATCOM) with a transmission bandwidth of only 10 to 50 kbps and latencies of 800 to 1,500 ms, **human sensory awareness is completely absent**. All engine situational awareness must be reconstructed from noisy, delayed, bandwidth-constrained telemetry.

### 1.2 The Deficiency of Conventional Avionics Monitoring
Conventional engine health monitoring systems (such as standard Engine Indication and Crew Alerting Systems - EICAS, or basic FADEC warning lamps) rely entirely on **static scalar threshold comparators**:
$$\text{Alert} = \begin{cases} \text{CRITICAL}, & \text{if } T_{\text{CHT}} > 135.0^\circ\text{C} \\ \text{NOMINAL}, & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$

This conventional approach possesses three structural engineering flaws:
1. **Zero Temporal Memory:** A Cylinder Head Temperature (CHT) of 134°C that has remained rock-steady for 6 hours is indistinguishable to a threshold comparator from a CHT that has surged from 95°C to 134°C in 90 seconds. The impending disaster lives in the first and second time-derivatives ($\dot{T}, \ddot{T}$), which static thresholds ignore.
2. **Zero Environmental Context:** At 25,000 ft over the Karakoram range in winter ($\text{OAT} = -35^\circ\text{C}$, air density $\rho = 0.54\,\text{kg/m}^3$), an engine producing 70% cruise power with a CHT of 125°C is in severe thermal distress due to the near-total lack of cooling mass-flow. Conversely, at sea level in Rajasthan during peak summer ($\text{OAT} = +48^\circ\text{C}$, $\rho = 1.10\,\text{kg/m}^3$), 125°C is entirely normal. A single global threshold must be set conservatively high to prevent nuisance alarms during takeoff in Rajasthan, rendering it blind to thermal runaway in Ladakh.
3. **Post-Damage Annunciation:** Redlines are engineered to mark the boundary where structural metallurgy is already degrading (e.g., aluminum alloy cylinder heads annealing and softening at >140°C). By construction, alerting at the redline means the failure has already initiated.

## 3. High-Priority Traceability Deficits & Target Solutions

From this line-by-line audit, **four core technical requirements** from PS 26054 require architectural elevation:

### 1. Plant-Observer Decoupling (DT-01 / FP-01..08)
* **The Reality:** Currently, `can_streamer.py` and `thermo_model.py` import the exact same thermodynamic code. Faults are injected by adding arithmetic deltas ($\Delta T = 40^\circ\text{C} \times \text{ramp}$).
* **Target Solution:** Decouple plant simulation from observer twin. The synthetic plant should run a higher-order numerical model (or replay real avionics logs), while the Digital Twin computes its baseline via the 1D thermodynamic observer. Generalize fault isolation so that fault signatures are evaluated dynamically rather than keyed to single-cylinder constants.

### 2. Vibration Channel Bandwidth (HM-06 / FP-08)
* **The Reality:** Gearbox vibration is transmitted in the primary telemetry stream at 20 Hz. By the Nyquist-Shannon sampling theorem, the maximum observable frequency is 10 Hz. The propeller reduction gear meshing frequency at 5,000 RPM is $102.8\,\text{Hz}$ ($i = 2.43$, 3rd harmonic), making real gear tooth FFT impossible at 20 Hz.
* **Target Solution:** Follow the edge architecture documented in `docs/study/06_vibration_analysis.md`: high-rate sampling (5 to 10 kHz) must occur on the edge SBC (e.g., Raspberry Pi or Jetson), where order tracking and envelope FFT extraction are computed locally. Only extracted scalar spectral features (harmonic ratios, RMS, sideband energy) are downlinked over the 20 Hz / 25 kbps telemetry stream.

### 3. Training Corpus Scale & Generalization (AI-01 / AI-02)
* **The Reality:** While the ML pipeline exhibits zero train/val/test mission overlap and records 97.5% accuracy, the entire dataset spans only **22.5 minutes of runtime** (30 missions × 45 seconds). Degradation trends cannot physically mature over 45 seconds.
* **Target Solution:** Execute long-duration simulation runs (30 to 120 minutes each) covering complete flight phases (`TAXI` $\to$ `TAKEOFF` $\to$ `CLIMB` $\to$ `CRUISE` $\to$ `LOITER` $\to$ `DESCENT` $\to$ `RECOVERY`), allowing true thermal settling and gradual mechanical wear accumulation.

### 4. Engine Architecture Realism: SI vs. CI Heavy Fuel (SR-03)
* **The Reality:** The Rotax 912 iS is a naturally aspirated, spark-ignition engine fueled by AVGAS 100LL. However, actual Indian MALE UAVs (TAPAS-BH-201 / Rustom-II) operate at 28,000 ft powered by turbocharged, common-rail direct injection (CRDi) diesel engines running Jet-A1 kerosene (Austro Engine AE300 / VRDE-Jayem 2.2L). A naturally aspirated engine cannot produce sufficient power above 12,000 ft.
* **Target Solution:** Maintain the Rotax 912 iS as the primary spark-ignition benchmark, but introduce configuration profiles in `thermo_model.py` supporting turbocharged heavy-fuel compression-ignition parameters (manifold boost up to 2.2 bar, common-rail injection timing up to 1,600 bar). This aligns the solution with the actual Indian Armed Forces operational theater.
