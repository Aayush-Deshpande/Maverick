## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

### 1.1 What We Actually Built
We have constructed a **modular software prototype and simulation testbed** designed to evaluate engine health monitoring and tactical mission reliability for MALE UAV aero piston engines:
* **Backend:** A high-concurrency FastAPI service (`backend/services/state.py`) running a stable 20 Hz state-update tick loop, processing physics, state estimation, and anomaly detection in **4.2 ms** (leaving 45.8 ms of idle headroom within the 50 ms window).
* **Physics Observer:** A lumped-parameter first-principles thermodynamic model (`backend/physics/thermo_model.py`) that calculates expected cylinder head and oil temperatures from RPM, throttle, airspeed, and altitude, generating **thermodynamic residuals** ($\Delta T = T_{\text{measured}} - T_{\text{expected}}$).
* **AI Diagnostic Layer:** An unsupervised NumPy Bottleneck Autoencoder (14-8-4-8-14) executing in 0.062 ms, coupled with a 100-tree Scikit-learn Random Forest classifier achieving 97.5% validation accuracy across 8 specific fault modes.
* **Prognostics Engine:** A statistical trend extrapolator (`backend/ml/trend_analyser.py`) utilizing Akaike Information Criterion (AIC) polynomial model selection and 500-sample Monte Carlo perturbation to compute Remaining Useful Life (RUL) with 90% confidence intervals, alongside Palmgren-Miner / Rainflow thermal fatigue damage tracking.
* **3D Simulation & Auto-GCAS:** A 78.5 MB 109-part CAD model of the Rotax 912 iS with dynamic vertex-color thermal emission shaders in Blender, connected to a 6-DOF dynamic canyon flight simulator (`standalone_canyon_flight_app.py`) featuring an Automatic Ground Collision Avoidance System (Auto-GCAS) that executes emergency 3.5g terrain fly-up maneuvers when engine thrust degrades.
* **Ground Control Station:** A dark-themed React 18 + Three.js WebGL tactical interface (`frontend/`), integrated with an entirely local, air-gapped Voice Copilot (Whisper.cpp STT + local Qwen3-4B SLM + Kokoro TTS).

## 3. END-TO-END DATA FLOW TRACE

We traced the lifecycle of **Cylinder Head Temperature #2 ($T_{\text{CHT,2}}$)** from origin to dashboard:

1. **Origin (`backend/telemetry/can_streamer.py`):** $T_{\text{CHT,2}}$ is synthesized via numerical integration of fuel burn minus cooling airflow, plus an optional step-ramp fault offset (+35°C over 40 s).
2. **CAN Frame Packaging:** Encoded into CAN ID `0x0201` as a 16-bit unsigned integer with a scale factor of $0.1^\circ\text{C/LSB}$.
3. **Ingestion (`backend/services/state.py`):** Decoded at 20 Hz into a Pydantic `TelemetryRecord` with timestamp and validity bitmask.
4. **Range Gating (`backend/services/detection_pipeline.py`):** Checked against physical limits ($-40^\circ\text{C} \le T \le 250^\circ\text{C}$) and rate limits ($|\Delta T / \Delta t| \le 50^\circ\text{C/s}$).
5. **Physics Reference (`backend/physics/thermo_model.py`):** The model calculates expected nominal temperature $\hat{T}_{\text{expected}} = 109.5^\circ\text{C}$ based on current RPM (5,200), airspeed (105 kts), and altitude (8,500 ft).
6. **Residual Generation:** Residual $r_2 = 142.0 - 109.5 = +32.5^\circ\text{C}$. Normalized z-score becomes $r_2^* = +5.1\sigma$.
7. **Anomaly Scoring (`backend/ml/autoencoder.py`):** Residual vector $\mathbf{r}^*$ passes through the 14-8-4-8-14 bottleneck autoencoder. The reconstruction error breaches $\tau = 0.05$.
8. **Fault Classification (`backend/ml/classifier.py`):** The Random Forest evaluates decision splits. $r_2^* > 3.0$ with nominal oil pressure isolates **Fault F02 (Cylinder #2 Overheat / Exhaust Valve Leak)** with 98.2% confidence.
9. **Temporal Gating:** `MajorityVoteBuffer` requires the diagnosis to persist for 3 out of 5 frames ($150\text{ ms}$) before latching.
10. **Prognostics (`backend/ml/trend_analyser.py`):** Quadratic regression fits the slope of $r_2^*$, projecting that CHT2 will reach the critical softening limit ($150^\circ\text{C}$) in 18.4 minutes (90% CI: 14.8 to 22.1 min).
11. **WebSocket Dispatch:** Packaged into a JSON state payload dispatched over `ws://localhost:8000/api/v1/telemetry/stream`.
12. **Frontend UI Update (`frontend/src/`):** React renders the CHT dial in flashing red, updates the alarm banner, and Three.js alters the fragment shader emission on `Cylinder_Head_02` from dark blue to incandescent red.

## 5. AUDIT OF THE DIGITAL TWIN CLAIM

### Is It a Digital Model, Digital Shadow, or Digital Twin?
Under formal aerospace standards (**AIAA S-119-2011**, **ISO 23247**, and **NASA-STD-7009A**):
* **Digital Model:** Static 3D mesh or simulation with manual data exchange.
* **Digital Shadow:** One-way automated telemetry pipeline from physical asset to digital entity.
* **Digital Twin:** Bi-directional automated coupling where digital twin analytics automatically command physical actuators.

**Forensic Verdict:** Our system is currently a **High-Fidelity Digital Shadow operating at STANAG 4586 Level of Interoperability (LOI) 2 [PARTIALLY IMPLEMENTED]**.
* **Why this is defensible:** In military aviation, closing the loop to allow experimental AI to command engine throttle in flight violates **RTCA DO-178C** airworthiness certification. A read-only advisory architecture (Digital Shadow) is the correct, airworthy operational posture.
* **What is missing for a true Digital Twin:** Hardware-in-the-Loop dynamometer feedback where software commands adjust dyno load resistance in a test cell.

### Mathematical Credibility of `RotaxThermoModel`
* **Equations Modeled:** First Law of Thermodynamics applied to lumped control volumes:
  $$m_{\text{head}} c_p \frac{dT_i}{dt} = \eta_{\text{thermal}} \dot{m}_{\text{fuel}} \text{LHV} \xi_i - h_{\text{conv}}(V_{\text{IAS}}, \rho) A (T_i - T_{\text{amb}})$$
* **Physical Realism:**
  * **Altitude & Density:** Models barometric pressure lapse and air density scaling, reducing convective heat dissipation at high altitudes.

* **Scientific Limitations [SIMULATED]:**
  * Does not model localized 3D combustion flame propagation or finite-element cylinder head thermal gradients.
  * Engine RPM is prescribed by throttle input rather than dynamically emerging from a crank-propeller torque balance equation ($I_{\text{prop}} \frac{d\omega}{dt} = Q_{\text{eng}} - Q_{\text{aero}}$).

## 9. FAULT & DEGRADATION INJECTION AUDIT

All 8 DRDO fault modes are explicitly modeled in `backend/ml/generator.py` and `can_streamer.py`:
1. **Fault F01 (Combustion Misfire):** Cylinder health factor $\xi_i \to 0.0$. Fuel flow continues but unburned fuel causes EGT to drop rapidly while 0.5X torsional vibration spikes.
2. **Fault F02 (Cylinder Head Overheat):** Convective cooling area $A_{\text{fin}}$ reduced by 40% (simulating cowling baffle collapse) or exhaust valve leaking combustion gases into casting. CHT rises at $+0.8^\circ\text{C/s}$ to $>145^\circ\text{C}$.
3. **Fault F03 (Cooling System Degradation):** Coolant pump cavitation or radiator obstruction. Radiator effectiveness $\varepsilon_{\text{rad}}$ drops by 50%. All 4 cylinder heads climb uniformly.
4. **Fault F04 (Oil Pressure Loss / Lubrication):** Oil pump relief valve sticking or line puncture. $P_{\text{oil}}$ drops from 4.5 bar to 1.2 bar while friction heat causes $T_{\text{oil}}$ to climb rapidly.
5. **Fault F05 (Sensor Bias / Drift):** Linear ramp bias injected into a single thermocouple pin ($+0.5^\circ\text{C/s}$). Physics observer flags single-channel divergence while adjacent cylinders remain nominal.
6. **Fault F06 (Combustion Instability):** Rapid cycle-to-cycle pressure variations. EGT oscillates with $\sigma > 25^\circ\text{C}$ and 1X crank vibration increases.
7. **Fault F07 (Severe Overheating / Pre-Ignition):** Runaway thermal spike exceeding $150^\circ\text{C}$ on multiple cylinders.
8. **Fault F08 (Abnormal Mechanical Vibration):** Gearbox dog-clutch wear or propeller blade imbalance. Gear mesh vibration amplitude jumps from 0.8g to 4.2g.
