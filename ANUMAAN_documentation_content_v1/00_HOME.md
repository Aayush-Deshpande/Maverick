# Project ANUMAAN

## AI-Powered Real-Time Digital Twin for Aero-Piston Engine Health & Mission Reliability

**SIH Problem Statement:** 26054  
**Team:** Midnight Ciphers  
**Project:** ANUMAAN

> A cyber-physical engineering platform for synchronizing an aero-piston engine model with telemetry, estimating expected behaviour from physics, detecting deviations, diagnosing fault modes, tracking degradation, and translating engine health into mission-level consequences.

## What is ANUMAAN?

ANUMAAN combines:

- a deterministic engine / plant simulation layer,
- physics-based expected-state estimation,
- telemetry validation and residual generation,
- anomaly detection and fault diagnosis,
- degradation and Remaining Useful Life (RUL) estimation,
- mission reliability analysis,
- 3D digital-twin visualization,
- historical replay and simulation,
- and an operator-facing web GCS.
